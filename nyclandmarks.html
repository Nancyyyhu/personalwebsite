Book Design - 50 Ways In

This project collects fifty tickets from across the city—luxury stores, museums, restaurants, and entertainment venues—as small admissions into different moments of urban life. Each ticket becomes a fragment of experience, a record of entry into a slightly different version of the city.

Together, they form a personal archive of New York: not a clear map of the city, but a constellation of memories, impressions, and fleeting admissions.
