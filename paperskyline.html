Identity Design - Paper Skyline

This project presents a conceptual visual proposal for New York City’s landmarks through a combination of editorial design and digital experience. Inspired by the city’s iconic skyline and architectural diversity, the project reinterprets familiar landmarks using a pop-up book format. Architectural forms rise from the pages as paper structures, transforming the act of reading into an interactive exploration of urban space.

Alongside the book, a companion website extends the project into a digital environment, presenting the landmarks through motion and navigation. Together, the printed and digital components form a unified design proposal that celebrates the spatial, cultural, and visual identity of New York.
